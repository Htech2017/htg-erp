HTG ERP - MO THU MUC GOOGLE DRIVE TREN MAY (Windows)

Dung khi may da cai Google Drive cho may tinh (Drive for desktop) va muon cac nut
"mo thu muc" trong phan mem mo thang File Explorer thay vi mo tren web.

CAI DAT (moi may lam 1 lan)
1. Giai nen thu muc nay, chay install.bat.
2. Nhap duong dan thu muc GOC cua he thong tren may (thu muc ben trong co "Orders"),
   vi du  G:\Shared drives\HTG\ERP
   Duong dan nay phai giong duong dan Admin da nhap o trang Cai dat he thong.
3. Mo phan mem, bam mot nut mo thu muc bat ky, chon "Mo tren may" (chi hoi 1 lan).

SU DUNG
- Bam nut mo thu muc bat ky: File Explorer mo dung thu muc cua don.
- Thu muc chua dong bo ve may thi mo thu muc cha gan nhat.
- Khong thay cua so mo: bam "Mo tren web" trong thong bao o goc man hinh.

AN TOAN
- Cong cu chi mo THU MUC nam trong thu muc goc da khai bao, khong chay chuong trinh nao.
- Go bo: chay uninstall.bat.

May khong cai cong cu nay (hoac Mac) van dung binh thuong: nut mo thu muc se mo tren web.
